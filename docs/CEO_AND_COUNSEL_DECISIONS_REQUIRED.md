## Clauses Requiring Counsel Approval

- Privacy notice: data categories, purposes, lawful bases, disclosures, processors, rights,
  retention, children/minors, security statements, international transfers, and contact.
- Terms: legal entity, governing law, acceptable use, healthcare/AI limitations, warranties,
  indemnity, liability, IP, suspension, termination, disputes, and changes.
- Cookie policy: actual technologies, consent categories, analytics, advertising, retention,
  regional behavior, and withdrawal.
- Healthcare AI disclaimer: intended use, clinician oversight, emergency language, device and
  connector limits, patient-facing use, and escalation.
- Accessibility and refund statements.
- FaithCore consent and clinical-neutrality language.
- Any customer, outcome, partner, investor, certification, regulatory, security, privacy, or
  deployment claim.

## Decision Record Template

For each ID, retain:

- named signer and organizational role;
- exact approved text or option;
- pages and jurisdictions in scope;
- evidence and contract references;
- decision and conditions;
- effective and review/expiry dates;
- counsel identity and approval reference;
- candidate/source fingerprint to which the decision applies.

Until recorded, interim legal pages must retain the legal-review banner and commercial
healthcare deployment remains blocked.
