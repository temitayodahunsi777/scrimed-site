## Investable Thesis

SCRIMED is building a governed healthcare work and intelligence platform. Its defensible value is
not exclusive access to one model. The thesis is that healthcare buyers need model-independent
workflow execution with evidence, provenance, policy, human review, interoperability, and
measurable accepted outcomes. SCRIMED's architecture is designed to retain those assets when
providers, models, prices, regions, or deployment modes change.

## Investor Communication Rule

External investor material must remain bound to an exact source and artifact fingerprint and
receive founder, finance, legal, and recipient-specific approval. It must distinguish implemented
local foundations, synthetic evidence, external validation still required, production state,
and prohibited claims. No system may initiate outreach or make securities, valuation, customer,
partnership, revenue, certification, or clinical-impact representations autonomously.
