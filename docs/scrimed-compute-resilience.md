# SCRIMED Compute Resilience

Compute Resilience tracks provider availability, accelerator class, region, data-residency eligibility, capacity, concentration, export controls, power and cooling risk, cost volatility, replacement lead time, and fallback readiness.

Deployment profiles cover cloud, private cloud, customer VPC, sovereign cloud, on-premises, air-gapped, and edge environments. No protected workload may move silently; every fallback requires renewed policy, residency, privacy, security, safety, capacity, and approval evaluation.

Strategic monitoring covers accelerators, foundries, advanced packaging, semiconductor testing, HBM, networking, power, cooling, export restrictions, and regional infrastructure. Scores are planning metadata, not infrastructure certification, availability assurance, purchase authority, or production authorization.
