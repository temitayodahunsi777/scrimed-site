# CEO-Directed Public Remediation Plan

## Objective

Align every SCRIMED-controlled public surface with the current company posture: pre-commercial, Atlas-first, faith-neutral by default, synthetic-data/demo-first, no-PHI, human-supervised, and nonclinical.

## Implemented In This Candidate

- Central company identity with no published street address until a verified record exists.
- Fail-closed operating mode for PHI, live care, devices, emergency monitoring, EHR connections, autonomous treatment, eligibility, payer decisions, and faith-influenced clinical logic.
- Validation and Evidence and interim Legal and Policy Center routes.
- Atlas-first homepage, qualified planning ranges, optional FaithCore pathway, and public healthcare/AI disclaimers.
- Public intake operating-mode gate, no-PHI consent, server validation, markup rejection, rate limiting, bounded routing, and minimum-necessary business fields.
- Central public-claims policy, build-aware release verification, and CI enforcement.

## External Actions

The Wix marketing site remains separately authored and must be corrected in Wix Editor. Static testimonial, address, store/navigation, vitals, legal, metadata, and form changes require owner preview and publication review. See `docs/EXTERNAL_SYSTEM_ACTIONS_REQUIRED.md`.

## Retained Hard Stops

No code in this candidate authorizes PHI, live clinical decision-making, diagnosis, treatment, emergency monitoring, device ingestion, EHR writeback, payer submission, production deployment, certification claims, or customer go-live.

## Decision Boundary

Engineering may enforce and verify these limits. The CEO must approve binding commercial posture, verified business identity, material spend, production activation, fundraising terms, and any public claim backed by nonpublic company evidence.
