# SCRIMED Global Certification Readiness

Updated: 2026-06-25

## Purpose

Global Certification Readiness prepares SCRIMED for future domestic and international healthcare operation by turning approval, certification, privacy, security, AI governance, medical-device, clinical-safety, cyber, procurement, and localization requirements into owned evidence work.

Primary surfaces:

- `/global-certification-readiness`

## Boundary

This lane is readiness planning only. It does not grant legal advice, regulatory approval, HIPAA compliance certification, SOC 2/HITRUST/ISO certification, FDA clearance, ONC certification, EU AI Act conformity, GDPR compliance assurance, NHS approval, MHRA approval, Australian cyber certification, PHI processing authority, public-sector procurement approval, reimbursement assurance, production connector approval, or live clinical care authority.

## Official Source Map

- HHS HIPAA Security Rule: administrative, physical, and technical safeguard evidence for ePHI readiness.
- FDA Clinical Decision Support Software Guidance: intended-use, non-device CDS, and device escalation analysis.
- FDA AI in Software as a Medical Device: lifecycle management, premarket pathway, change control, transparency, and monitoring implications.
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework: AI inventory, risk taxonomy, measurement, evaluation, incident response, and TrustOS evidence language.
- EU Artificial Intelligence Act: high-risk AI triage, documentation, data governance, logs, human oversight, robustness, cybersecurity, accuracy, and post-market planning.
- European Commission GDPR framework: lawful basis, DPIA, DPA/SCC, data-subject rights, retention, transfer, and role mapping.
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023: AI management system policy, traceability, transparency, risk treatment, audit cadence, and continuous improvement.
- NHS DTAC: clinical safety, data protection, technical security, interoperability, usability, and accessibility buyer evidence.
- MHRA Software and AI as a Medical Device roadmap: intended purpose, classification, clinical evidence, post-market surveillance, change management, cybersecurity, and AIaMD escalation.
- Australian Cyber Security Centre Essential Eight: application control, patching, MFA, privilege, backup, and hardening evidence for Australian buyer diligence.

## Operator Routine

1. Keep all public workflows synthetic-only until privacy, PHI, clinical, security, and production approvals exist.
2. Assign an owner to each track and gate before buyer-facing claims expand.
3. Store evidence as metadata-only route references unless an approved protected vault is activated.
4. Route HIPAA, GDPR, FDA, MHRA, EU AI Act, NHS, Australia, SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO, and public-sector claims through qualified external review.
5. Add a smoke assertion before a new approval or certification surface is treated as buyer-critical.
