Boundaries:

- Enterprise Scalability Operations is not a contractual SLA or uptime guarantee.
- It is not a managed service commitment, SOC/MDR coverage, or 24/7 production support commitment.
- It does not approve production hosting, data residency, disaster recovery posture, production connectors, or customer-specific tenancy.
- It does not authorize PHI processing, live clinical care, EHR writeback, or production clinical workflow execution.
- It does not provide legal, accounting, tax, audit, revenue, or profit-margin advice.
- Support tiers, response targets, regional claims, disaster recovery language, and SLO/SLA wording require qualified review before external use.

Operator routine:

1. Start with `/enterprise-scalability` before any buyer asks for scale, tenant growth, uptime, response time, 24/7 support, residency, DR, or cost commitments.
2. Attach capacity assumptions, route class, queue/backpressure controls, rollback path, and smoke coverage before scaling buyer-critical routes.
3. Confirm tenant owner, role matrix, AAL2 path, access review cadence, offboarding trigger, and archive owner before workspace expansion.
4. Keep SLO candidates internal until counsel, finance, service reliability, and customer operations approve exact external language.
5. Route support tier, escalation path, response target, incident communication, and renewal cadence through Client Onboarding and Enterprise Business Ops.
6. Route residency, sovereign hosting, regional deployment, and DR claims through Global Certification Readiness and Deployment Profiles.
7. Route model cost, storage, support labor, custom diligence, and implementation scope through Enterprise Business Ops before proposals expand.
8. Promote repeated scale defects into Service Reliability, Operational Efficiency, Boundary Resolution, or public smoke coverage.

Release checks:

- Public smoke verifies `/enterprise-scalability`, `/api/enterprise-scalability`, and `/api/enterprise-scalability/brief`.
- API headers must preserve no-SLA, no-managed-service, no-PHI, no-live-care, no-production-connector, no-security-certification, no-revenue-guarantee, and no-profit-guarantee authority.
- Product Console must expose enterprise scalability route, proof-stack statuses, domain/control/workstream/cadence/bottleneck counts, and blocked claims.
- Boundary Resolution must include enterprise-scalability records and an operating rule before scale commitments expand.
- Operational Efficiency must include the enterprise-scalability domain and the enterprise scalability/support readiness sprint.
