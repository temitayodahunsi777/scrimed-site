# Enterprise Discovery Questionnaire

Status: **INTERNAL / NON-CLINICAL / NO-PHI INTAKE**

1. Which one to three workflows create the most delay, rework, review burden, or evidence gaps?
2. Who is the executive sponsor, operational owner, security/privacy owner, and final approver?
3. What are the current steps, handoffs, cycle time, exceptions, abandonment points, and volumes?
4. Which data sources are involved, and can the work proceed with synthetic or de-identified
   operational metadata only?
5. Which actions are read-only, reversible, consequential, or prohibited?
6. What baseline, outcome, safety, adoption, burden, latency, and cost measures are available?
7. Which integrations are required for demonstration, and which must remain simulated?
8. What evidence would support `STOP`, `NEXT_SYNTHETIC_WORKFLOW`, or `EXTEND_SYNTHETIC_PILOT`?
9. Which legal, security, privacy, procurement, accessibility, or residency constraints apply?
10. What decision must the sponsor make from the executive readout?

Do not submit PHI, patient records, credentials, secrets, live claims, or regulated customer data.
