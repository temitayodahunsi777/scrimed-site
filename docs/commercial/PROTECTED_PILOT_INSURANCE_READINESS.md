# Protected Pilot Insurance Readiness

No insurance coverage is evidenced or claimed by this repository. The canonical reviewer register is `docs/review/P34_INSURANCE_READINESS_REGISTER.md`.

| Coverage | State | Evidence needed | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CYBER | `NOT_EVIDENCED` | Current binder or policy schedule, limits, exclusions, retroactive date, incident duties | Founder, finance, counsel |
| TECH_EO | `NOT_EVIDENCED` | Current binder or policy schedule, covered services, limits, exclusions, claims-made terms | Founder, finance, counsel |
| GENERAL_LIABILITY | `NOT_EVIDENCED` | Current binder or policy schedule and applicable exclusions | Founder, finance |
| OTHER_COUNSEL_REQUIRED | `NOT_EVIDENCED` | Counsel/broker gap analysis for D&O, employment, media, crime, or specialty coverage | Founder, counsel |

Allowed states are `NOT_EVIDENCED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, and `VERIFIED`. `VERIFIED` requires documentary evidence, named review, effective dates, and expiry monitoring. A quote, application, or verbal statement is not coverage evidence.

Protected-pilot activation remains blocked until applicable insurance, counsel, security/privacy, customer agreement, integration, AAL2, Supabase, deployment, commercial, and production-specific gates are satisfied.
