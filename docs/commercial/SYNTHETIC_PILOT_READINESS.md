# SCRIMED Synthetic Workflow Pilot

The synthetic pilot is SCRIMED's bounded commercial evaluation path before protected or production work. It is no-PHI, nonclinical, nonproduction, evidence-generating, reversible, and human-controlled.

## Lifecycle

`DISCOVERY -> WORKFLOW_MAPPING -> BASELINE -> SYNTHETIC_SCENARIOS -> CONTROLLED_CONFIGURATION -> EVALUATION -> EVIDENCE_PACKET -> EXECUTIVE_READOUT -> EXPANSION_DECISION`

Only the next stage may be entered. Missing scope, evidence, synthetic data, or measurable outcomes blocks progression. No stage grants production authority.

## Canonical Commercial Posture

- Workflow Intelligence Assessment: starting at $25K, subject to written agreement.
- SCRIMED Synthetic Workflow Pilot: custom enterprise scope.
- Protected Enterprise Pilot: custom enterprise scope subject to security, privacy, insurance, deployment-readiness, and written authorization requirements.

Agents may draft scope, calculate synthetic scenarios, and prepare nonbinding proposals. They cannot sign, quote, accept terms, commit dates, negotiate liability, authorize PHI, or authorize production.

## Evidence

Every result links scenario, model, agent, workflow, evaluation, metric, and evidence source. Packs carry the watermark `SYNTHETIC / NON-PRODUCTION` and bind scenario, candidate, model, configuration, and evaluation versions. Value Returned and Verified Intelligence Yield are assumption-bound synthetic indicators, not customer, clinical, revenue, margin, or ROI claims.

## Budgets

Default limits cover model spend, tool calls, runtime, retries, and agent depth. Any exceeded limit blocks execution. The pilot router may consider cost, quality, latency, and task fit, but cannot lower privacy or safety floors.

## Activation Boundary

Pilot preparation can proceed internally. Binding amount, partnership, customer commitment, material scope, liability, PHI, and production decisions require named human authority. Protected pilot, customer activation, production connector, payer submission, EHR/device writeback, live clinical use, and public outcome claims remain blocked.
