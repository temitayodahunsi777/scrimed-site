# Enterprise AI Governance Pilot

Status: **INTERNAL DRAFT / HUMAN COMMERCIAL AND CLAIMS APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE EXTERNAL SEND**

SCRIMED helps an enterprise inventory its approved AI models, agents, tools, workflow uses, data
classes, owners, approval gates, and evidence obligations in a bounded synthetic/no-PHI pilot.

## Deliverables

- model, agent, and tool inventories with version and ownership fields;
- workflow and risk classification;
- policy, approval, escalation, rollback, and kill-switch matrix;
- evidence graph and auditability assessment;
- cost and routing governance review;
- release-governance scorecard and executive risk summary.

## Success Measures

Coverage, ownership completeness, unresolved high-risk controls, evidence freshness, reviewer burden,
policy adherence, and cost per completed governed workflow. Results are `SIMULATED`, `ESTIMATED`, or
`UNAVAILABLE` until supported by approved evidence.

## Commercial Boundary

Custom enterprise scope, subject to a written agreement and named human approval. SCRIMED agents may
draft scope and pricing scenarios but cannot sign, accept terms, promise dates, authorize PHI,
activate a protected pilot, or distribute the packet externally.

This pilot grants no compliance, certification, production, clinical, customer, or partnership
claim and does not authorize model/provider activation.
