# Workflow Intelligence Assessment Sales Pack

Status: **INTERNAL DRAFT / HUMAN COMMERCIAL APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE EXTERNAL SEND**

## One-Page Offer

SCRIMED maps one to three healthcare operational workflows using no PHI, identifies bottlenecks, handoffs, rework, cycle-time burden, evidence gaps, and bounded automation opportunities, then delivers an executive decision packet and synthetic-pilot recommendation.

- Starting point: `$25,000`, subject to written scope and named human approval.
- Typical scope: 30 days, one executive sponsor, one operational owner, one to three workflows.
- Inputs: process documentation, de-identified operational metrics, stakeholder interviews, and synthetic scenarios.
- Outputs: current-state map, friction register, evidence-gap analysis, automation candidate scorecard, governance requirements, success metrics, and executive readout.
- Boundaries: no PHI, diagnosis, treatment, payer submission, EHR/device writeback, production activation, guaranteed savings, or binding proposal without approval.

## Discovery Questionnaire

1. Which workflow creates the most delay, rework, or review burden?
2. Who owns the workflow and who approves change?
3. What are the baseline steps, cycle time, handoffs, exceptions, and abandonment points?
4. Which data sources are involved, and can the assessment proceed without PHI?
5. What evidence would make a synthetic pilot worth funding?
6. Which security, privacy, legal, procurement, or integration constraints apply?
7. What decision must the executive sponsor make at the end of 30 days?

## Synthetic SOW Outline

1. Parties and nonbinding draft status.
2. Buyer alias, sponsor, operational owner, and SCRIMED delivery owner.
3. In-scope workflows and explicit exclusions.
4. No-PHI and nonproduction data boundary.
5. Deliverables, review cadence, acceptance criteria, and evidence classification.
6. Fixed fees, expenses, change control, and payment terms approved by humans.
7. Confidentiality, data handling, IP, warranties, liability, termination, and governing law for counsel review.
8. No clinical, payer, EHR/device, customer-activation, or production authority.

## Executive Readout

`Problem -> Baseline -> Intervention -> Controls -> Evidence -> Synthetic Result -> Limitations -> Estimated Value -> Cost -> Risk -> Recommendation -> Expansion Decision`

Expansion decisions are limited to `STOP`, `NEXT_SYNTHETIC_WORKFLOW`, `EXTEND_SYNTHETIC_PILOT`, or `PREPARE_PROTECTED_PILOT`. The last option grants no activation.

## Success Metrics

- workflow steps and handoffs;
- cycle time and rework;
- accepted-output and correction rates;
- reviewer burden and abandonment;
- policy adherence and evidence completeness;
- model/tool cost and cost per accepted output;
- time returned and estimated cost avoided, labeled `ESTIMATED` or `SIMULATED` until verified.
