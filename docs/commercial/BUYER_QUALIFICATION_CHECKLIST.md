# Buyer Qualification Checklist

Status: **INTERNAL / HUMAN COMMERCIAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

Score each dimension with cited business evidence: pain, urgency, pilot fit, no-PHI feasibility,
data burden, integration burden, regulatory burden, commercial potential, expansion potential, and
strategic value.

## Required Checks

- named executive and operational owners;
- one bounded workflow and measurable baseline;
- synthetic/no-PHI path available;
- decision and success criteria defined before execution;
- review capacity and evidence owner available;
- security/privacy/procurement constraints disclosed;
- budget and timeline are hypotheses until authorized;
- prohibited claims, actions, integrations, and data are explicit;
- protected/production expectations are separated from synthetic evaluation.

Output exactly one: `HIGH_PRIORITY`, `QUALIFIED`, `NURTURE`, or `NOT_FIT`, with reasons and evidence.
Qualification is not a promise, contract, credit decision, clinical decision, or customer activation.
