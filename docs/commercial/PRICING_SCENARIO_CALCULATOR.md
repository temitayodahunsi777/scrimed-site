# Pricing Scenario Calculator

Status: **INTERNAL / NONBINDING / HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED**

## Inputs

- approved offer and scope fingerprint;
- workflow count, scenario count, duration, and delivery capacity;
- model, retrieval, tool, infrastructure, engineering, reviewer, support, and contingency cost;
- expected accepted-output rate and retry/correction burden;
- minimum margin policy, change-control reserve, and proposal expiry.

## Outputs

- estimated direct and total delivery cost;
- cost per completed workflow and accepted output;
- estimated price, margin, break-even, and sensitivity range;
- assumptions, excluded work, evidence class, approver, and pricing fingerprint.

Workflow Intelligence Assessment may use its separately approved public starting point. Synthetic
pilots, Protected Enterprise Pilots, and Enterprise Operating Licenses remain **custom enterprise
scope**. This calculator cannot quote, discount, sign, promise dates, accept terms, or authorize
protected/production work.
