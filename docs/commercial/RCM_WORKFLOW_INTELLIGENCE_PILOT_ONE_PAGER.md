# RCM Workflow Intelligence Pilot

Status: **INTERNAL DRAFT / HUMAN COMMERCIAL AND CLAIMS APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE EXTERNAL SEND**

SCRIMED models one bounded revenue-cycle workflow with synthetic/no-PHI scenarios to identify
handoffs, documentation gaps, claim-quality issues, denial patterns, policy-mapping gaps, rework,
cycle time, and review burden.

## Permitted Scope

- synthetic workflow and claim-quality simulation;
- documentation-completeness analysis;
- coding-recommendation simulation for human review;
- denial-root-cause and payer-policy mapping;
- evidence packet, executive readout, and next synthetic-workflow recommendation.

## Success Measures

Steps, handoffs, cycle time, rework, accepted outputs, corrections, reviewer minutes, policy
adherence, evidence completeness, model/tool cost, and cost per accepted output. Financial value is
an `ESTIMATED` or `SIMULATED` hypothesis until verified under an approved measurement plan.

## Hard Stops

No real claim submission, appeal, coding mutation, payer-system write, payment adjustment,
collections activity, medical-necessity assertion, PHI, production connector, or autonomous
financial decision is authorized.

Pricing is custom enterprise scope, nonbinding, and subject to written human approval.
