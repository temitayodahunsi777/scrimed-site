# SCRIMED Continuous Review, Audit, and Innovation

Updated: 2026-06-25

SCRIMED Continuous Review, Audit, and Innovation is the operating lane for 24/7 agent-assisted review, evidence checks, mistake reduction, incident learning, and future research assignment.

## Operating Boundary

This lane improves accuracy and readiness, but it does not provide managed 24/7 SOC/MDR coverage, autonomous production remediation, legal advice, security certification, regulatory approval, PHI processing authority, clinical care authority, public quantum capability claims, product commitments, investment advice, or permission to bypass human review.

## Review Agents

- Accuracy Review Agent samples product copy, API summaries, briefs, workflow outputs, and buyer-facing claims.
- Evidence Attribution Agent checks sources, reviewed dates, proof routes, documentation updates, and missing-source tickets.
- Claims and Boundary Agent blocks unsupported authority language and routes legal, clinical, certification, ROI, or customer-reference claims to review.
- Security Drift Agent tracks dependencies, headers, auth gates, protected route behavior, and post-quantum readiness signals.
- QA Regression Agent turns repeated failures into smoke, typecheck, lint, build, and fail-closed assertions.
- Incident Learning Agent converts incidents, near misses, buyer questions, and support gaps into root-cause notes and prevention tasks.
- Innovation Scout Agent tracks future AI, healthcare infrastructure, interoperability, privacy, and quantum-safe signals for internal research only.

## 24/7 Loops

- Always-on sentinels: route health, boundary headers, proof-stack posture, and protected fail-closed paths.
- Accuracy sampling: daily samples and high-risk buyer-output review.
- Evidence attribution sweep: source support, reviewed dates, proof routes, and missing-source routing.
- Claims and authority guard: release and buyer-packet checks against prohibited claims.
- Security and dependency drift review: dependency, auth, cryptography, route, and protected access drift.
- Human escalation and remediation: owner assignment, fix validation, evidence preservation, and residual-risk notes.
- Innovation horizon scan: future signals turned into research assignments and promotion gates.
- Governance rehearsal: quarterly review of repeated defects, open risks, audit gaps, and research bets.

## Internal Innovation Tracks

- Quantum-Safe and Post-Quantum Readiness remains internal-only. The work is dependency inventory, vendor posture, key lifecycle mapping, NIST post-quantum standards tracking, buyer question preparation, and blocked-claim control.
- Agentic Review Automation prototypes review queues, confidence scoring, auto-test suggestions, and human approval checkpoints.
- Privacy-Preserving Compute compares synthetic data, de-identification, confidential compute, federated evaluation, and differential privacy under no-PHI constraints.
- Clinical Simulation and Synthetic Safety Lab adds adversarial synthetic scenarios and specialty workflow safety evaluation without diagnosis or treatment claims.
- Interoperability and Sovereign Deployment Futures tracks FHIR, SMART, DICOMweb, X12, IHE, terminology, residency, and regional procurement signals.

## Promotion Gates

- Human review must remain in place for production changes, clinical language, certification claims, public buyer claims, and security-sensitive actions.
- Quantum-safe messaging cannot become public without security-owner approval, vendor posture evidence, external review, and claim-guard approval.
- Repeated defects should become deterministic smoke assertions, route inventory updates, source refresh tasks, blocked claims, or owner-assigned prevention tasks.
- No loop may ingest production PHI, store secrets, bypass AAL2, approve live care, certify security/compliance, or perform autonomous production remediation.
