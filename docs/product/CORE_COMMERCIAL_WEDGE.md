# Core Commercial Wedge

**Decision status:** internal recommendation awaiting founder commercial adoption
**Primary wedge:** Workflow Intelligence Assessment
**Demonstration workflow:** Documentation Before Authorization

## Buyer And Pain

The initial buyer is an operations, revenue-cycle, access, documentation, or transformation
leader who needs to understand workflow friction before authorizing an integration. The pain is
missing documentation, rework, unclear ownership, preventable delay, and weak evidence about
where automation is safe and economically useful.

## Controlled Intervention

1. Map one bounded workflow using no PHI.
2. Establish buyer-owned baseline definitions.
3. Run deterministic synthetic scenarios.
4. Show model routing, agent roles, policy denials, evidence, cost, and human review.
5. Deliver an evidence packet and prioritized implementation decision.

## Measurable Baseline

- workflow volume supplied by the buyer;
- minutes per workflow;
- handoffs, rework, and missing-information rate;
- reviewer burden;
- accepted-task definition;
- current unit cost and delay assumptions.

No baseline or improvement is fabricated. Values remain unmeasured until a buyer approves the
definitions and evidence.

## Proof And Effort

Current proof routes are `/offerings`, `/documentation-before-authorization`,
`/healthcare-value-realization`, `/validation-evidence`, and `/scrimed-control-plane`. A standard
engagement is designed to be fixed-scope, no-PHI, synthetic, and reversible.

## Nonbinding Packaging

- Workflow Intelligence Assessment: starting at $25,000; final scope and pricing require a
  written agreement.
- Synthetic Pilot: custom enterprise scope.
- Protected Integration Pilot: custom scope after separate identity, migration, security,
  privacy, legal, clinical, buyer, and release gates.

The software may prepare scope and ROI scenarios but cannot bind price, discount, delivery,
contract, pilot activation, or outcome commitments.

## Expansion Path

Assessment -> governed synthetic evaluation -> protected workflow pilot after gates -> annual
governed workflow subscription after measured acceptance and contract approval.

No live PHI, payer submission, EHR writeback, diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, customer
activation, or production deployment is authorized by this commercial path.
