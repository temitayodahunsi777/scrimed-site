## Core Wedge

SCRIMED's near-term entry wedge is the existing **Workflow Intelligence Assessment**, with
**Documentation Before Authorization** as the strongest product workflow for demonstrating the
full governed platform.

The controlled progression is:

1. Paid no-PHI workflow assessment.
2. Governed synthetic evaluation.
3. Protected workflow pilot after identity, data, security, migration, and reviewer gates pass.
4. Annual governed workflow subscription only after validated acceptance and contract approval.

This sequence creates a credible route to revenue evidence without prematurely requiring PHI,
production connectors, payer submission, EHR writeback, or live clinical authority.

## Stop Rules

- Do not create a new standalone product when an existing workflow, control, or route can carry
  the requirement.
- Do not promote marketplace, live connector, PHI, clinical, payer, EHR, or device capability
  before its explicit gate passes.
- Do not count synthetic demonstrations as customers, outcomes, revenue, validation, or traction.
- Retire unsupported claims immediately; retire reusable infrastructure only through evidence,
  dependency, export, recovery, and rollback review.
