## Boundary

This lane is operating-readiness material only. It is not legal advice, accounting advice, tax advice, audited financial reporting, securities offering material, investment advice, valuation assurance, contract approval, revenue guarantee, profit-margin guarantee, reimbursement assurance, customer permission, certification, PHI processing authority, production connector approval, or live clinical care authorization.

- SCRIMED Capital Vitality, Growth Engine, and Public Market Readiness: internal revenue, growth, KPI, margin, board, and finance-methodology readiness.
