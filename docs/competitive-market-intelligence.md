## Clean-Room Market Response

SCRIMED may learn from public market patterns, but it must implement original SCRIMED tools, workflows, evidence packets, copy, metrics, and user experience. The clean-room rule is simple:

- Allowed: high-level public patterns, buyer expectations, category gaps, workflow themes, diligence expectations, proof disciplines, and market positioning lessons.
- Blocked: third-party code, UI, screenshots, customer logos, customer proof, proprietary workflows, private APIs, model weights, datasets, pricing tables, report artifacts, certification claims, partner claims, or regulated clinical claims.
