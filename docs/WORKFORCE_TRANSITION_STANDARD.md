# Workforce Transition Standard

SCRIMED evaluates human-plus-AI workflow changes before any employment consequence is
considered. Automation output cannot authorize a staffing decision.

A proposal is review-ready only when it includes:

- completed workflow redesign;
- a training and upskilling plan;
- documented redeployment consideration;
- measured workload impact;
- a clinician-time protection plan; and
- scheduled transparent human review.

Any proposed role elimination remains blocked as an external employment authority decision,
even when every workflow condition is met. Human review is always required. This standard does
not promise job retention, authorize workforce changes, provide employment advice, or replace
labor, legal, clinical, finance, or organizational review.
