Boundaries:

- Company Assessment is not legal advice, accounting advice, tax advice, audited financial reporting, investment advice, securities offering material, solicitation, or valuation assurance.
- Company Assessment is not HIPAA, SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO, FDA, ONC, EU AI Act, GDPR, NHS, MHRA, Australian cyber, or regional certification.
- Company Assessment is not security assurance, penetration-test authorization, attack-proof guarantee, or managed SOC/MDR coverage.
- Company Assessment does not authorize PHI processing, production connectors, EHR writeback, payer submission, patient matching, patient outreach, diagnosis, treatment, triage, or live clinical care.
- Company Assessment does not create customer permission, public launch approval, contractual SLA, uptime guarantee, revenue guarantee, profit-margin guarantee, reimbursement assurance, or public-market readiness assurance.
- Company Assessment does not authorize public quantum, autonomous-remediation, production model-routing, live autonomous AI, accessibility certification, or trillion-scale equivalence claims.

Operator routine:

1. Start at `/company-assessment` before launch, buyer, investor, service, board, approval, platform, health-record, or protected proof decisions.
2. Review the overall score, readiness band, watch lanes, upgrade-now lanes, external-review lanes, and protected-gated lanes.
3. Review whole-company audit findings for the clearest strength to amplify, gap to close, revenue unlock, and risk to control.
4. Pick the revenue builder that matches the buyer and route it to package, margin, conversion, and proof controls.
5. Pick the competitive edge amplifier that should be made more apparent in public copy, demos, pilots, or investor packets.
6. Pick the highest-priority improvement item and assign owner, proof route, and next action.
7. Pick the highest-risk weakness relief item and assign the owner, proof route, and next action.
8. Pick the highest-risk missing capability closure and choose the current workaround, permanent build, blocked-until condition, owner, and proof route.
9. Pick the highest-value revenue or product workstream and confirm the retained boundary before external use.
10. Route legal, privacy, security, certification, clinical, PHI, reimbursement, regional, accounting, tax, valuation, securities, or investment questions to qualified review.
11. Route buyer communications through `/client-onboarding`, paid work through `/service-delivery`, packaging through `/offerings`, and enterprise business terms through `/enterprise-business-ops`.
12. Route API, UI, AI, model-route, agent, eval, retrieval, and cost questions through `/platform-power`.
13. Route health-record and interoperability questions through `/health-records` and `/clinical-authority-readiness`.
14. Route PHI, live-care, connector, clinical AI, certification, global production, customer go-live, or clinical-production language through `/clinical-production-readiness`.
15. Route approval and certification work through `/approvals-readiness` and `/global-certification-readiness`.
16. Route repeated blockers through `/limitations-workarounds`, `/operational-efficiency`, and `/boundary-resolution`.
17. Route protected buyer proof release through `/qa-buyer-proof-release`, `/buyer-release-control-run`, and `/pilot-workspace/access`.
18. Update Product Console, Hub, Navigation Audit, README, systems map, and public smoke when a company-critical route becomes buyer-critical.

Missing capability closure rules:

- Every missing capability must include why it matters, current workaround, permanent build, owner, proof routes, success metric, blocked-until condition, and retained boundary.
- Critical closures currently cover production tenant/SSO activation, live connector authority, clinical validation and regulated claims, and external security/compliance evidence.
- High-priority closures currently cover buyer proof release automation, support/SLA readiness, evidence vault governance, and model evaluation/red-team benchmarking.
- Medium-priority closures currently cover CRM/billing/accounting automation and investor KPI/data-room discipline.
- Missing capability closure does not waive retained gates; it converts a gap into controlled current action plus a permanent build path.
