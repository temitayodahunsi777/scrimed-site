# SCRIMED Operational Efficiency

Updated: 2026-06-27

SCRIMED Operational Efficiency is the cross-system control plane for gaps, inefficiencies, bottlenecks, hard stops, proof routes, owners, and resolution sprints. It turns scattered release, route, reliability, onboarding, scalability, platform-power, commercial, enterprise, audit, and boundary constraints into one execution map.

Current posture:

- Source lanes: release continuity, navigation audit, service reliability, client onboarding, enterprise scalability, platform power, limitations workarounds, growth engine, enterprise business operations, continuous review/audit, and boundary resolution
- Discrepancy and fault triage queue: route/API/smoke drift, protected auth status mismatch, claims boundary drift, buyer handoff stalls, scale/support overcommit, interoperability live-data slip, agent autonomy overreach, and finance margin leak
- Resolution sprints: release and route preflight, commercial throughput, client onboarding and communication cadence, enterprise scalability and support readiness, API/UI/AI platform power, limitations workaround containment, continuous defect-to-control loop, health-record extraction and safety, and enterprise margin/control discipline
- Efficiency metrics: open bottleneck pressure, proof-route density, hard-stop visibility, and sprint execution readiness
- Data boundary: synthetic and metadata only

Boundaries:

- Operational Efficiency is not autonomous production remediation.
- Operational Efficiency does not bypass AAL2 or qualified human review.
- Operational Efficiency does not process PHI, authorize live clinical care, certify security or accessibility, send email, create calendar invites, approve contracts, create public API SLAs, create contractual SLAs, guarantee uptime, approve live autonomous AI, approve production model routing, commit managed-service coverage, approve production hosting or residency, approve production connectors, approve buyer release, claim trillion-dollar-scale equivalence, or guarantee revenue, ROI, or profit margin.
- Legal, accounting, tax, audited financial, securities, certification, buyer-specific release, production connector, PHI, and live clinical care claims remain blocked until qualified human or external review is retained.

Operator routine:

1. Route each newly discovered gap, repeated defect, sales bottleneck, release blocker, or margin leak into `/operational-efficiency`.
2. Confirm the record has a domain, owner, hard stop, proof route, current control, and next action.
3. Route blocked requests through `/limitations-workarounds` so each workaround has a packet, owner, proof route, expiration rule, and graduation gate.
4. If a signal conflicts across app, API, docs, Product Console, protected auth, claims, buyer handoff, scale, live-data, agent, or finance surfaces, select the matching discrepancy and fault triage item first.
5. Confirm immediate containment, root-cause probe, permanent control, owner, proof routes, and promotion trigger before release or buyer use.
6. Promote deterministic recurring issues into smoke coverage, route inventory, service reliability controls, workaround packets, or boundary records.
7. Use the nine efficiency sprints to sequence work instead of relying on informal memory.
8. Keep buyer-facing or investor-facing claims behind Product Console, Boundary Resolution, Enterprise Business Ops, and qualified review gates.
9. Preserve the no-PHI, no-secret, no-autonomous-remediation boundary whenever proof routes or protected operator work are referenced.
