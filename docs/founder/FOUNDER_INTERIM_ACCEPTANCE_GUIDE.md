# Founder Interim Acceptance Guide

Founder interim acceptance is limited to an exact, hash-bound, synthetic-only preproduction
candidate. It can cover a source commit, nonproduction preview, synthetic demo, internal model
evaluation, Wix copy remediation, disposable migration validation, reversible configuration, and
continued precommercial development.

It cannot authorize PHI, clinical execution, production migration/deployment, EHR/device access,
customer go-live, binding legal adoption, regulated claims, diagnosis, treatment, payer action, or
emergency monitoring. The acceptance expires in 30 days or immediately after any invalidating
change listed in `founderInterimAcceptance.ts`.

1. Verify the exact candidate and assurance manifest fingerprints.
2. Review automated controls, AI-assisted lanes, residual risks, and operator actions.
3. Select only permitted activities.
4. Sign through a trusted identity method; do not paste a signature or token into the repository.
5. Store only the verification receipt and fingerprint in the approved evidence system.

AI review supports this decision but cannot sign it or impersonate the founder.
