## Operating Routine

1. Name the competitor and use only public, official source material.
2. Identify the SCRIMED weakness the competitor exposes.
3. Write the SCRIMED counter-position in original language.
4. Attach a proof route, owner, and next hardening move.
5. Route every legal, privacy, security, customer-proof, certification, PHI, and clinical claim through the correct qualified review gate.
6. Treat every infiltration risk as prevention, detection, response, evidence route, and hard stop.

## Required Boundaries

- Competitive Defense does not copy competitor products, code, workflows, datasets, customer proof, security claims, regulatory claims, or branding.
- Competitive Defense does not provide legal advice.
- Competitive Defense does not authorize PHI processing.
- Competitive Defense does not certify security, privacy, compliance, SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO, HIPAA, FDA, ONC, GDPR, EU AI Act, NHS, MHRA, or regional approval status.
- Competitive Defense does not authorize penetration testing or third-party testing.
- Competitive Defense does not assert competitor partnerships or product parity.
- Competitive Defense does not guarantee protection from attack.
- Competitive Defense does not approve customer evidence release or external distribution.
- Competitive Defense does not authorize live clinical care.

## Infiltration Deterrence Pattern

Each layer must include:

- Likely attack path.
- Prevention control.
- Detection signal.
- Response action.
- Evidence route.
- Hard stop.

Current layers cover public API surface, protected tenant workspace, agent tool execution, health-record/interoperability pathways, claims/commercial language, and build/dependency pipeline.

## External Review Gates

- Counsel-reviewed claims and competitor comparison.
- Privacy and HIPAA risk analysis.
- Security assessment and penetration-test authorization.
- Customer release and evidence distribution.

These are review gates only. They must be completed by qualified human reviewers before public claims, production data use, security claims, penetration-test claims, or external evidence distribution.
