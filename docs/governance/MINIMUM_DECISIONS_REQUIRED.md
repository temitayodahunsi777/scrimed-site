# Minimum Decisions Required

Only decisions that cannot safely or lawfully be automated remain here.

## Founder Interim Decisions

| Decision | Trigger | Why external | Safe default | Recommendation | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accept exact candidate for bounded preproduction use | Clean candidate and assurance packet | Founder bears product authority | Do not preview/publish | Accept only listed synthetic activities | Before preview |
| Authorize nonproduction preview | Preview package ready | External platform action | Local demos only | Approve expiring synthetic preview | Before investor demo |
| Choose public address posture | Public identity review | Verified identity fact unavailable | Publish no street address | Keep no-address posture until verified | Before legal adoption |
| Authorize Wix copy publication | FaithCore copy staged | Public publication is owner-controlled | Leave gate open | Publish exact neutral copy | Before broad outreach |
| Authorize limited operator actions | Supabase/Wix/CI packet ready | Requires platform identity | No mutation | Approve one scoped action at a time | As needed |

## Targeted Specialist Decisions

| Decision | Trigger | Why external | Safe default | Recommendation | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adopt legal policies | Binding publication/contract | Licensed legal judgment | Interim drafts only | Qualified counsel reviews exact text | Before commercial adoption |
| Activate PHI | Proposed PHI workflow | Privacy/security authority | No PHI | Review purpose, contracts, region, retention | Before any PHI |
| Approve clinical intended use | Consequential clinical workflow | Clinical accountability | Decision support disabled | Review exact workflow and evidence | Before activation |
| Accept production security risk | Production promotion | Named risk ownership | No production promotion | Review threat model and recovery | Before deployment |
| Authorize production migration | Dry-run evidence complete | Database production authority | No migration | Approve exact hashes/window/recovery | Before migration |
| Approve regulatory classification | Regulated intended use proposed | Specialist interpretation | No regulated claim | Obtain specialist opinion | Before claim/use |
| Authorize production release | All applicable gates complete | Release authority | No deployment | Bind environment/window/rollback | Before deployment |

## Commercial Decisions

Customer contracts/go-live, material spending, fundraising, equity, and binding partnerships remain
founder/authorized-signatory decisions. The safe default is no commitment or customer activation.
Evidence preparation may continue, but sending, signing, spending, or activating requires the
appropriate authority at the transaction point.
