# Founder-Led Preproduction Review Policy

## Scope

This interim policy applies while SCRIMED is pre-commercial, synthetic-only, no-PHI,
nonclinical, non-emergency, and non-production. It reduces review ambiguity without replacing
qualified professional accountability.

The founder may authorize continued development, synthetic demonstrations, internal testing,
non-production previews, reversible repository changes, non-binding product strategy, operator
preparation, and disposable-database testing.

The founder may not substitute for qualified legal, clinical, privacy, cybersecurity,
regulatory, database-production, or deployment approval when professional or operational
accountability is required. Founder authorization grants no PHI processing, clinical action,
production migration, production deployment, payer submission, EHR or device connection,
certification claim, or customer go-live authority.

## Required Label

Every decision under this policy must state:

> Founder interim acceptance for pre-production development only.

The decision must identify the exact candidate/source/validation/SBOM fingerprints, scope,
allowed activities, prohibited activities, expiration, and evidence reviewed. A template is in
`docs/governance/FOUNDER_INTERIM_ACCEPTANCE_FORM.md`.

## Expiration

Acceptance expires at the earliest of 30 days or any architecture, model, data-boundary,
clinical-intended-use, deployment-environment, database-migration, security-control, or public-
claims change. Expiration returns the affected action to fail-closed review.

## Required Human Review

Qualified human sign-off remains mandatory for legal-policy adoption, regulatory strategy,
clinical safety, privacy policy, production security risk acceptance, production database
migration, production deployment, PHI activation, binding agreements, and customer go-live.
AI-assisted review may prepare evidence but cannot satisfy those gates.
