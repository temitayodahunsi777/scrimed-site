# SCRIMED Product And Services Portfolio

Updated: 2026-06-26

The Product and Services Portfolio turns SCRIMED's product proof into a canonical packaging layer for offers, packages, delivery playbooks, margin controls, proof routes, and retained healthcare boundaries.

## Routes

- Page: `/offerings`
- JSON API: `/api/offerings`
- Markdown brief: `/api/offerings/brief`
- Demo and pilot commercial accelerator: `/pilot-demo-commercial-readiness`
- Accelerator API: `/api/pilot-demo-commercial-readiness`
- Accelerator brief: `/api/pilot-demo-commercial-readiness/brief`
- Delivery workbench: `/service-delivery`

## Operating Rule

Every buyer conversation should resolve to one package, one offer, one proof route, one margin control, and one retained boundary before pricing, pilots, diligence, implementation, or enterprise-license work expands.

Before a demo or pilot pricing call, route through `/pilot-demo-commercial-readiness` so the buyer path has one selected demo, one recommended pilot package, one price band, one proof asset list, one no-PHI intake route, one market benchmark context, and one retained hard-stop boundary.

After an offer is selected, route it through `/service-delivery` so scope, work orders, acceptance criteria, artifacts, buyer handoffs, margin protections, and retained no-PHI/no-SLA/no-contract gates are explicit before kickoff.

## Boundary

This portfolio is product, service, and commercial operating readiness only. It is not a signed quote, contract, procurement approval, discount approval, legal advice, accounting advice, tax advice, audited financial reporting, securities offering material, investment advice, valuation assurance, customer permission, revenue guarantee, profit-margin guarantee, ROI guarantee, reimbursement assurance, clinical validation, compliance certification, security certification, PHI processing authority, production connector approval, EHR writeback approval, payer submission approval, or live clinical care authorization.
