# SCRIMED Approval Achievement Engine

## Purpose

The Approval Achievement Engine converts broad readiness lists into a dependency graph with evidence, owners, missing artifacts, human sign-offs, external authorities, expiration, safe commercial workarounds, blocked actions, and revenue unlocks.

## One Gate Completed

The **No-PHI Control Plane Automated Technical Quality Gate** is achieved for the dated working-tree evidence scope. It is backed by the control-plane contract, full nonsecret suite, typecheck, lint, 445-page build, compiled smoke, generated integrity, workspace hygiene, and diff checks. It expires after material source change or the stated weekly refresh date.

This is an internal automated engineering gate only. It is not legal, privacy, clinical, regulatory, security-assurance, buyer, PHI, production, certification, or customer go-live approval.

## Fastest Human Approval Candidate

The proposed Intended Use Memo is present at `docs/SCRIMED_INTENDED_USE_MEMO.md`. To achieve internal Intended Use approval:

1. Founder/CEO reviews the intended use, users, outputs, exclusions, and public messaging boundary.
2. Qualified legal reviewer confirms claims and regulatory escalation triggers.
3. Clinical governance reviewer confirms clinician oversight, escalation, and no-autonomous-care boundaries.
4. Record reviewer identities, decisions, dates, version, and expiry outside source code.
5. Align website, demos, decks, sales scripts, investor materials, and pilot scopes to the approved version.

No system or agent may self-approve this memo.

## External Paths

- HIPAA/BAA: formal risk analysis, safeguards, data flow, vendor, incident, agreement, and customer authority.
- SOC 2: readiness assessment, control remediation, evidence window, management assertions, and independent CPA examination.
- FDA/CDS: module-level intended-use and device/non-device classification under current FDA guidance.
- ONC/HTI: connector-specific certification applicability, standards, algorithm-transparency, customer acceptance, monitoring, and rollback.
- EU: current counsel review of AI Act role/risk, GDPR lawful basis/DPIA, DPA/SCC, residency, transparency, human oversight, and documentation.
- UK: product-specific DTAC, clinical safety, privacy, security, interoperability, accessibility, usability, MHRA, and buyer review.
- Australia: Essential Eight maturity, privacy, hosting, procurement, health-sector, and buyer review.

Official-source snapshots are dated and require legal confirmation before decisions. They are evidence inputs, not legal advice or approval.
