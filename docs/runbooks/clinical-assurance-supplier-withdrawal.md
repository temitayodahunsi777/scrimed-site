# Supplier Withdrawal Runbook

## Trigger

Use for provider/model withdrawal, ownership or policy change, license restriction, sanctions/export constraint, security compromise, price shock, or sustained capacity degradation.

## Controlled Procedure

1. Record the machine-readable supplier event and evidence reference.
2. Suspend new traffic when severity requires reauthorization.
3. Identify affected model, host, cloud, region, serving pool, identity, network, storage, and subcontractor edges.
4. Verify the alternative route is materially independent across every material dimension.
5. Confirm exact passport, artifact signature, scans, domain-cell eligibility, contracts, CAL, enclave, and capacity.
6. Rotate affected credentials and keys through the approved external procedure.
7. Restore signed exported configuration and artifacts in the alternate environment.
8. Transfer bounded queues; send unresolved work to human review.
9. Reconcile CaseEvidence and audit hash continuity.
10. Measure recovery against declared RTO/RPO and obtain incident closure approval.

The local drill records expected decisions only; no external supplier, queue, credential, key, or production system is changed.
