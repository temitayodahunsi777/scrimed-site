# SCRIMED Verification and Governance

Completion requires mandatory schema, evidence, citation, freshness, consistency, policy, PHI, prohibited-action, scope, budget, loop, rollback, and human-approval checks. An agent's own narrative never counts as completion evidence.

The Trust Score measures evidence quality and coverage, freshness, policy compliance, context completeness, model agreement, citation completeness, hallucination risk, reversibility, and human-review status. A high score cannot bypass a failed mandatory check.

The Reasoning Observatory stores structured operational metadata only: objective, plan, current step, accepted and rejected evidence, assumptions, unresolved questions, contradictions, risk, policy, model route, tools, verification, and approval. It does not expose or retain hidden chain-of-thought.

Objective drift toward speed over safety, completion over correctness, token savings over evidence, revenue over patient benefit, or automation over governance pauses the session and requires review.
