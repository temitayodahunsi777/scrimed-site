# SCRIMED ConsequenceBench

ConsequenceBench extends the synthetic Clinical Benchmark Suite with consequence dimensions: clinical severity, financial exposure, privacy exposure, reversibility, affected-population risk, underrepresentation risk, and detection difficulty.

Reports separate average, high-consequence, worst-group, edge-case, abstention, unsupported-claim, false-reassurance, human-override, rollback, and distribution-shift performance. Missing scores remain zero and are labeled awaiting evidence; they are not presented as model failure or validation.

Clinical and protected workflows require qualified human graders. ConsequenceBench does not establish clinical validation, regulatory clearance, production authority, or customer readiness.
