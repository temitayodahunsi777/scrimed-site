# SCRIMED Strategic Roadmap

## Now

- Create one clean exact-source candidate and independent review packet.
- Rehearse Workflow Intelligence Assessment for three buyer archetypes.
- Capture synthetic baseline, acceptance, reviewer-burden, latency, and cost evidence.
- Complete real AAL2 and disposable migration evidence without production activation.

## Next

- Obtain counsel-reviewed fundraising and commercial authority boundaries.
- Run one buyer-approved synthetic evaluation with explicit acceptance criteria.
- Qualify provider and connector adapters through conformance and failure testing.
- Commission privacy and security readiness when a qualified buyer requires protected scope.

## Later

- Advance a protected no-PHI pilot only after identity, migration, tenant, reviewer, and buyer
  gates pass.
- Build renewal evidence from approved cases and measured outcomes.
- Expand sovereign and edge profiles only where measured demand supports their operating cost.

## Optionality

- Retain the clinical experience catalog as synthetic demonstrations.
- Keep the partner marketplace deferred until one read-only adapter passes governance,
  security, legal, and commercial validation.
- Keep production PHI and device integration prohibited until separately approved.

The roadmap prioritizes evidence and buyer learning over feature count. It is advisory and grants
no production, commercial, clinical, regulatory, partnership, investment, or distribution
authority.
