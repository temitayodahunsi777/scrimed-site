# SCRIMED Guided Execution Path

SCRIMED Guided Execution Path is a synthetic/no-PHI control plane that maps each audience to the right demo, proof route, pricing motion, follow-up artifact, and retained boundary.

## Why It Matters

SCRIMED now has many strong proof surfaces. The next strategic bottleneck is audience clarity: buyers, investors, clinics, partners, pilot leads, and internal operators need different paths through the same platform.

Guided execution turns route depth into a repeatable sales, diligence, pilot, and operating system.

## Audience Paths

The registry currently covers:

- hospital buyer
- faith-based clinic
- investor
- pilot lead
- implementation partner
- internal operator

Each path includes:

- landing route
- demo sequence
- proof routes
- pitch angle
- pricing motion
- trust objections handled
- required artifacts
- success metrics
- human next action
- retained boundary
- audit hash

## Sales And Investor Use

Guided execution helps SCRIMED tell a tighter story:

- what to show first
- which proof routes support the claim
- what offer or pricing motion follows
- which trust objections are already handled
- what the buyer or investor should do next

This improves sales performance and investor confidence without making unsafe clinical, regulatory, customer, or financial claims.

## Safety Boundary

Guided execution does not authorize live PHI, autonomous clinical care, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, patient outreach, payer submission, EHR writeback, production connector approval, certification claims, investment advice, or customer go-live.
