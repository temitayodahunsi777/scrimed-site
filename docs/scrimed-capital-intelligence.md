# SCRIMED Capital Intelligence Copilot

Capital Intelligence is internal-only. It can research investor archetypes, score healthcare and AI thesis fit, assess portfolio overlap, identify geographic and sovereign relevance, track objections, prepare diligence answers, and draft internal memos.

It cannot contact investors, grant data-room access, share confidential files, discuss valuation, negotiate terms, communicate projections, represent commitments, or send binding communications. All outbound activity requires explicit CEO approval plus applicable legal, finance, accounting, tax, securities, and claims review.

Scores prioritize internal research. They do not imply interest, endorsement, fundraising success, valuation, or investment suitability.
