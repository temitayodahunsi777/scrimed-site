# Finance And Cost-Control Review

**Disposition:** AI REVIEW PASS WITH CONDITIONS. A named finance reviewer remains required for
external economic claims or material spend.

## Controls Reviewed

- Ten governed agent teams default to a $5 synthetic-run ceiling, ten-minute runtime, 24 actions,
  two retries, and a two-failure circuit breaker.
- Model routing records cost/token/latency/tool/retry/fallback budgets and stops on exhaustion.
- Maximum effort requires human authorization; unavailable providers cannot create fallback
  loops or weaken safety tier.
- Cost is attributed to task outcomes and reviewer corrections, not presented as audited ROI.
- External provider concentration and contract economics remain unverified until documentary
  review.

## Evidence Classification

All current modeled value and cost examples are `SIMULATED` or `ESTIMATED`. No `VERIFIED` ROI,
savings, collections, clinical benefit, customer outcome, or investor return is claimed. Unknown
values remain `UNAVAILABLE`.

## Conditions

Before any commercial/investor economic claim, Finance must review assumptions, provider price
date, infrastructure/retrieval/retry/reviewer costs, customer baseline, confidence interval,
claim wording, publication permission, and expiry. Material spend requires founder authority.
